01 / LEARN BY DOING

A bridge between DAWs and trackers.

The precision of tracker sequencing with a more approachable visual model. Contour is designed to connect the familiar ideas of timelines and piano rolls to the spreadsheet-like commands and patterns that make trackers powerful.

See what the spreadsheet is doing.

Learn through Song, Chain, Roll and Phrase views. Reuse ideas, follow musical structure, and understand how notes, effects and modulation translate into sound.

Instead of hiding meaning behind hexadecimal commands, the goal is to make their behaviour visible so beginners can learn the workflow and experienced tracker musicians can work quickly.

Horizontal timeline thinking
Visual feedback
Reusable phrases
Hardware-first controls

02 / SOUND DESIGN

Small synths. Big personalities.

Inspired by the instrument variety of Korg Gadget, Contour's cluster synthesizer model explores a collection of focused engines instead of one intimidating all-purpose synth.

SYNTH / 01

Character first

Analogue-inspired, FM, phase distortion, wavetable and experimental flavours, each with an identity of its own.

SYNTH / 02

Fast to edit

Compact controls, consistent parameter layouts and focused engines that reward experimentation.

SYNTH / 03

Room to go deep

Macros, envelopes, modulation, samples, drum engines and effect routing when you want more detail.

The synthesizer family is expanding during development; engine availability will be documented by release.

03 / PERFORMANCE + VJ

A rave or demoparty in your pocket.

Contour is not only for editing songs. The performance vision combines live arrangement control, macros and effects with generative visuals and VJ-style scene manipulation, on a handheld you can carry anywhere.

Play it live

Shape sound on the fly

05 / CONNECTED MUSIC

Make it. Share it. Pass it on.

Contour's planned decentralised, StreetPass-style ecosystem explores direct exchange of songs, instruments, presets and artwork between devices, with room for discovery, attribution and remix histories.

A music community shouldn't need to depend entirely on one central platform. Sharing and creator ownership are key parts of the long-term vision.

09 / WHY CONTOUR EXISTS

From a Game Boy to Contour.

I found my way into making electronic music through LSDj and a Game Boy. A small handheld became a sequencer, synthesizer and an unexpected entry point into a whole creative world.

Contour is my way of carrying that opportunity forward — with approachable tracker learning, characterful instruments, visuals and affordable hardware. I want to keep building it for the community well beyond the first release.

If it helps somebody discover music the way LSDj helped me, it will have done something meaningful.

— Aisjam
